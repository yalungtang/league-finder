# AI usage

- ChatGPT was used to interpret the assignment, refine the product scope, explore UX copy, and develop the visual direction.
- Stitch was used to explore early UI/UX approaches. Those initial directions were then refined through first-round visual iterations with ChatGPT Image before the final design was selected.
- ChatGPT Image was used to create the generic multi-sport artwork shown in the catalogue empty state. It does not represent a real team or league.
- OpenSpec was used during the initial delivery phase to turn the approved design and PRD into a structured proposal, specification, implementation plan, and first working version. Its planning artifacts were intentionally excluded from the final repository.
- After that initial version was approved, the remaining visual, copy, responsive, routing, loading-state, caching, and interaction polish was completed directly with OpenAI Codex, without continuing the OpenSpec workflow.
- OpenAI Codex was also used to scaffold and implement the Vue application, integrate TheSportsDB, write tests, configure linting and formatting, and validate the responsive UI.
- The human developer directed the scope, reviewed the API mapping and generated output, selected the final visual treatment, and made the final product decisions.
- Runtime league names, metadata, badges, and league-specific fanart come from TheSportsDB rather than generated fixtures.
